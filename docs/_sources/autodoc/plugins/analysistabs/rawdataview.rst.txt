RawDataView
===========

**class RawDataView()**

Bases: :ref:`MetaEventTabView`

Subclass of MetaEventTabView for visualizing raw signal data and PSD plots.

Handles plot rendering, signal responses, and interactions with readers, filters, and event finders.
